FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which version of After Effects do I need?

After Effects 2020 or newer, up to 2026. Almost every tool works on all of them; the panel tells you on the tile when one needs a newer version (a few OS Kit tools need 2022 or 2024).

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. MotionSaas runs on both macOS and Windows, and the download includes an installer for each.

How do I download and install it?

Click any Download button to get MotionSaaS.zip and unzip it. On a Mac, double-click “Install on Mac.command” (the first time, right-click → Open). On Windows, double-click “Install on Windows.bat”. Restart After Effects and open Window → Extensions → MotionSaaS.

How do updates work?

The panel checks for a new version when it opens. When there is one, an Update button appears in its header — click it and MotionSaas downloads, installs and reloads itself. No reinstall.

Is MotionSaas free?

Yes, while it’s in early access — every tool is unlocked. Pricing may come later; we’ll announce it on this page first.

Will it mess with my project?

No. Everything MotionSaas adds is labelled, so Remove takes it off cleanly, and re-applying a tool updates what it made instead of stacking copies. It never overwrites expressions you wrote yourself.

Do I need any coding or expression knowledge?

No. Every tool is applied directly from the MotionSaas panel with one click — no expressions or manual keyframing required.
